# Modern logo provenance

`mig33-logo.png` retains the historical cyan/orange bubble mark and its custom
white lettering. It is a brand image; controls, gradients and other interface
decoration remain native components.

- Downloaded without modification on 2026-10-31 from
  <https://pinasthikaartista.files.wordpress.com/2015/02/mig33.png>.
- Source article:
  <https://pinasthikaartista.wordpress.com/2015/02/22/logo-vector-operator-seluler-indonesia/>.
- PNG: 320×182, RGBA, 15,231 bytes; transparent padding is retained.
- SHA-256: `5ca22dc09c3a6ad3d6894f4c55145c999535c76087d6a6ab32021f4bd80cbbb7`.

The mark was visually compared with theme122 from the hash-verified publisher
4.62 JAR (`dbf12ffbd44bc6e48d09cf35863736731c87c078877f76ed4b780a4dc5eff92c`).
That reference is an 80×45 PNG with SHA-256
`a06d07264a29d53d5f406b32a13acd07b69df780a9448d8eff7ea0692fcca56a`, recorded in
[`theme.json`](../../../research/applets/v4/docs/data/theme.json) and visible in
the original welcome43 capture. Both use the cyan gradient silhouette, custom
rounded lowercase white lettering, and raised orange “33” bubble. The larger
image is a secondary historical copy, not an authenticated publisher vector
export or a byte-identical enlargement of theme122. Its transparent overlap
and color rendering differ slightly from the small JAR image.

The file is used at a compact size in Modern, without font substitution,
background removal, recoloring, or generated detail. At 144 points wide it has
more than two source pixels per point; 3× screens modestly upscale it. Larger
variants are limited to Welcome. Classic logos and reference captures are
unchanged. A future publisher vector/master can replace this image without
changing the authentication interface.

Higher-resolution search results were inspected rather than trusted by their
labels: one contained a baked checkerboard; another added “malaysia”; a current
revival added “reborn”. None of those is bundled.
